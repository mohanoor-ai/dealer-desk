Other listings have no displayed rating until checked. Review text and publisher images are not copied. Ratings are a dated source snapshot and are not automatically refreshed. A different generation, unverified year or unknown model must not inherit a rating.

Storage choice design was checked against the ICO's [storage guidance on exemptions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/) and [pre-enabling optional storage](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-rules/). The implementation keeps optional viewed-history persistence off before a choice; this reference is not a legal compliance certification.
