# Documentation rules

Agreed with the owner on 24 October 2026. Apply these when updating front pages, diagrams, evidence and later articles.

1. Use plain descriptions of the problem, implementation and actual results. Remove inflated claims and generic AI-generated promotion.
2. Use real browser screenshots with fictional data. Do not invent UI, figures, passing checks or dashboard results. Never include credentials, real customer records or private account details.
3. Record each screenshot's capture date, environment and application commit. Preserve required image attribution and distinguish stock illustrations from actual vehicle photos.
4. Put one readable product Overview near the top of the README. Keep detailed CI, telemetry and incident captures beside their evidence.
5. Keep architecture and workflow diagrams editable. Show current behaviour accurately; label future release, R2, observability and responder work as planned until verified.
6. Cite concrete source files, test results and workflow runs. A diagram, article or green build alone does not prove a complete live journey.
7. Keep private learning notes and raw lesson transcripts outside this public application repository. Customer/dealer screens should contain product information, not learner/tutor/assessment labels.
8. Do not claim a grade, user mastery, homework completion or past independent review without the relevant evidence. Public articles require owner review of personal reflections and publication scope.

See diagrams.md for current/planned diagrams and screenshot policy; media/README.md for the current image provenance; verification.md for checked results.
